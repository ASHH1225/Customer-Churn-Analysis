# Customer-Churn-Analysis
Project Using Power BI

AIM:
To find a comprehensive solution to elevate customer retention efforts and mitigate customer churn.

TOOLS USED:
MS Excel, Power BI

SUMMARY:
We have scrutinized customer demographics, account details, and the services offered by the company. The analysis has revealed a steady decline in subscription rates over time, as well as areas for potential improvement in our internet services.

CONCLUSION:
The overall suggestions include:-
-Increasing the tech support capacity for Fiber Optic customers and lower tech tickets per customer to 0.5.
-Increase sale of 1 and 2 year contracts by 5% each.
-Yearly increase of automatic payments by 5%.
